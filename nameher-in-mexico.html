Who this is for

You already did the brave thing. You left the US or the UK. You built a life here and still can’t decide what to do next.

You’ve got the mornings that belong to you. You’ve got the apartment. Maybe you’re building a house or thinking about it. You still wake up running errands for somebody in your own head.

The woman who got you out of there is still on duty. She was built to survive something that is over now, and she has never once been told she can stand down. So she keeps performing, and you keep calling it a decision you can’t make.

Give yourself three days to see who you’ve been acting as, name her, and decide whether she comes with you.

December 4 to 6

The three days

Friday evening, a full Saturday, and Sunday until early afternoon. Roughly a day and a half of your actual time, which is what makes it reachable whether you drive down from Cancún or fly in from somewhere else in Mexico.

Friday

The circle

Arrive any time from four. Nothing you need starts before six, so an afternoon flight and a drive after work both get you here in time. At 5:45 the doors close and the phones go away for the night.

Then each woman says out loud, to the room, who she has been performing as. Most have never said it to another person. Then dinner together, at a table booked for all of us where every woman orders her own, with no structure on it, because the bond goes deeper over food.

Saturday

Workshop and bodyshop

The morning is on paper. The roles, the performances, the patterns, in writing, shared only if you want to share it. Then a long lunch, together or on your own, at one of two places nearby I’ll point you to.

The afternoon moves to the water. We walk there in silence and work in your body, because you’ve been holding this for years and paper doesn’t reach it. The evening is free. You need a night to yourself.

Sunday

Playshop

What you noticed, turned into something you can hold. Your first draft of the woman you’ve been.

Then a full hour on exactly how the next twenty five days run, so you leave knowing what Christmas week will ask of you. Closing circle and photographs. There’s time to be back in Tulum or Cancún by evening, or on a late flight out.

Before you ask

Here are the practical things

Where exactly is it?

Central Playa del Carmen, within ten minutes of the beach, in a private room rather than a public floor. The address goes to the six women once the room is confirmed.

Do I need to stay in Playa?

No. Some women will drive in from Tulum or Cancún each day and sleep in their own beds. If you’re flying in from Mexico City, Mérida or anywhere else in the country, book three nights here and tell me, and I’ll send you three places I’d put my own mother in.

Is food included?

Coffee, tea, water, fruit and light snacks are in the room every day. Meals are yours, which means every woman eats what her body needs and nobody pays for a set menu she can’t eat. Friday dinner is a table booked for all of us, each woman ordering her own, and Saturday has a long lunch break with two places nearby I’ll send you to.

What if I can’t get there by six on Friday?

Arrival runs from four to 5:45 with nothing programmed in it. If traffic from Cancún or a delayed flight makes you late, message me and I’ll hold the circle a few minutes. You won’t miss the opening.

What happens if I fall behind in December?

Christmas week sits in the middle of this and I built it knowing that. The audio is recorded and waiting, so a day you miss costs you nothing. Nobody in this program is behind. The program also stays yours for life once day 28 is done, so a week you lose in December is a week you can take again in March.

Is this therapy?

This is identity work led by a mentor. It sits alongside therapy and it doesn’t replace it. If you’re in the middle of something acute, tell me when you message and I’ll be honest with you about whether December is the right month.

What happens after December 31?

You’ll have retired her, and nine days later you choose who comes next. That’s ChooseHer™ on January 9, and it’s my gift to this first circle. After that, what comes next is yours to decide, and I’ll tell you about it when you ask.
