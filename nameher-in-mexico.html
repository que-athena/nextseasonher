Who this is for

You already did the brave thing. You left the US or the UK. You built a life here and still can’t decide what to do next.

You have the mornings that belong to you. You have the apartment. Maybe you’re building a house or thinking about it. You still wake up running errands for somebody in your own head.

The woman who got you out of there is still on duty. She was built to survive something that is over now, and she has never once been told she can stand down. So she keeps performing, and you keep calling it a decision you can’t make.

Give yourself three days to see who you’ve been acting as, name her, and decide whether she comes with you.

What makes this different

Most retreats end at the airport.

You fly home full. Within a week the week has eaten it, and six months later the whole thing is a photograph. This one keeps going after you leave.

The three days in Playa are the opening days of NameHer, my twenty eight day program. The rest arrives daily on audio, wherever you are. The last day lands on New Year’s Eve.

December 4 to 6

The three days

Friday evening, a full Saturday, and Sunday until early afternoon. Roughly a day and a half of your actual time, which is what makes it reachable whether you drive down from Cancún or fly in from somewhere else in Mexico.

Friday

The circle

Arrive any time from four. Nothing you need starts before six, so an afternoon flight and a drive after work both get you here in time. At 5:45 the doors close and the phones go away for the night.

Then each woman says out loud, to the room, who she has been performing as. Most have never said it to another person. Dinner together afterwards with no structure on it, because the bond goes deeper over food.

Saturday

Workshop and bodyshop

The morning is on paper. The roles, the performances, the patterns, in writing, shared only if you want to share it.

The afternoon moves to the water. We walk there in silence and work in your body, because you have been holding this for years and paper does not reach it. The evening is free. You need a night to yourself.

Sunday

Playshop

What you noticed, turned into something you can hold. Your first draft of the woman you have been.

Then a full hour on exactly how the next twenty five days run, so you leave knowing what Christmas week will ask of you. Closing circle and photographs. There is time to be back in Tulum or Cancún by evening, or on a late flight out.

Meet your host

Hiyaaa! I’m Que Athena.

I’m an identity and reinvention mentor, and I live here in Playa.

I spent ten years building a bridal beauty business in Charlotte. Then my marriage ended, and then the business closed, and I got on a plane for what I was still calling a trip. Here is the part nobody warned me about. Leaving was the easy decision.

What took me far longer was noticing that I had brought her with me. The woman who held all of it together while a marriage was ending. She was competent and she was exhausted, and she was still running my life in a country where nothing was chasing me anymore.

I named her. Then I retired her. That work is what NextSeasonHer is, and NameHer is where it starts.

I’m forty, I’ve done this in six countries, and I have no interest in telling you to manifest anything. What I have for you is three days, five other women, and a name.

Before you ask

Here’s the practical answers

Where exactly is it?

Central Playa del Carmen, within ten minutes of the beach, in a private room rather than a public floor. The address goes to the six women once the room is confirmed.

Do I need to stay in Playa?

No. Some women will drive in from Tulum or Cancún each day and sleep in their own beds. If you are flying in from Mexico City, Mérida or anywhere else in the country, book three nights here and tell me, and I will send you three places I would put my own mother in.

What if I cannot get there by six on Friday?

Arrival runs from four to 5:45 with nothing programmed in it. If traffic from Cancún or a delayed flight makes you late, message me and I will hold the circle a few minutes. You will not miss the opening.

What happens if I fall behind in December?

Christmas week sits in the middle of this and I built it knowing that. The audio is recorded and waiting, so a day you miss costs you nothing. Nobody in this program is behind. The program also stays yours for life once day 28 is done, so a week you lose in December is a week you can take again in March.

Is this therapy?

This is identity work led by a mentor. It sits alongside therapy and it does not replace it. If you are in the middle of something acute, tell me when you message and I will be honest with you about whether December is the right month.

What happens after December 31?

My January cohort, Travel to Recognize Her, opens on the 11th. You will hear about it on Sunday afternoon in the room, once rather than three times, and there is no expectation that you take it.
