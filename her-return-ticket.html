    HerReturnTicket™

    Plan the trip that leaves you with a Return Ticket back to yourself.

    You already know you need to get away. What you may not have is a way to make it identity-shifting. Most trips end the moment you land back home. You unpack, open your inbox, and by Wednesday the old life has closed back around you.

    HerReturnTicket is the plan I wish I'd had before my first solo trip. It walks you through the trip I'd design with you myself: the season you're in, the place that meets you there, where you'll sleep and what you'll spend, what you'll release before you leave, and how you'll protect the shift once you're home.

    You'll leave with

    • Your season named, and a destination chosen for it
    • Your length, home base, budget, and pace decided
    • Your safety, packing, and people handled before you go
    • A defining experience and a release ritual planned
    • A landing plan for your first 48 hours home

    Your answers save as you go. When you finish, your plan prints as a boarding pass. Keep it, post it, and let it remind you who's coming home.
