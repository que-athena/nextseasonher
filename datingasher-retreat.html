Most women will. You'll meet the group at the virtual welcome party and in the group chat before you land, so nobody walks in a stranger. You'll leave with a group chat you don't want to mute.

Can I room with a friend?

For sure. Book separately, then tell us who you're rooming with. Early Bird guests choose first.

Do I need to live in Charlotte?

No. Most of the group comes from Charlotte, and every woman is welcome.

How do I get there?

Fly into Cancún (CUN). Your airport transfers are included. Once we confirm the retreat, we'll suggest a Charlotte flight so the girls can travel together.

What if the retreat doesn't go ahead?

You get back everything you've paid by Mar 1. That's why we ask you to wait on flights until we confirm.

Will there be men there?

No. This weekend is for you and the girls. The men can wait three days.

What should I pack?

Your packing and arrival guide arrives Apr 15. Short answer: swimsuit, something cute for Saturday, and an open mind. Leave the ex's hoodie at home.

Is travel insurance really required?

Yes. We'll share a few recommended providers, and you send proof with your waiver by Apr 1.
