Part one · His Effort

Did he show up like a husband?

Answer for what he did. Not for how it felt.

One

Did he lead?

He chose the plan, the time and the place, and made sure you got there and home.

Two

Did he make his intentions clear?

You know whether he's dating for marriage, because he said so in his own words.

Three

Was he curious about you?

He asked questions, listened, and remembered what you said.

Four

Did he talk about money, work and the people in his life with respect?

Calm and solution-minded is a yes. Complaining, blaming or negative is a no.

Then notice how the bill went

He paid, and it never became a question. He didn't ask, hint or wait to see what you'd do. This is the stronger signal, and it outweighs the one below.

He offered to pay. Polite, and still a question he handed to you. A man who offers is leaving the door open for you to pay.

Five

Did he follow up on his own?

He reached out after the date and moved toward a next plan without you prompting him.

    Part two · Her Mirror

    How did you show up?

    No score here. This part is for you, and it gets clearer over several dates.

    Leading

    Did he ask you out and then say, "Let me know where you want to go and when"?

    Did he offer to assist you with getting there or getting home?

    Did you accept his help?

    Intentions

    Did he volunteer his readiness without you asking?

    When he shared his intentions, did your body feel reactive rather than settled?

    Do you want to go out with him again regardless of his intentions?

    Being known

    Did you feel resistance to him asking about you?

    When he asked about you, did you talk more about other people, like coworkers, family or exes?

    Receiving

    Did you offer to pay?

    Did he allow you to pay?

    Did you feel odd about him paying?

    Did you feel like you would owe him something if he paid?

    After

    Did you text him after the date?

    Did he ask you to?

    Did you feel butterflies after leaving him?

    Did you feel a strong desire to NOT see him again despite enjoying the date?
