What it doesn’t cover

Your travel, your lodging and your meals. All three of those are yours.

I’m saying it this plainly because a woman who wins a seat she can’t actually get to has won nothing, and I’d rather you know that before you spend twenty minutes writing to me.

This is an event rather than a stay, which is why it works for women already living here. Playa, Cancún and Tulum are a drive. Mérida, Mexico City, Guadalajara and Puerto Vallarta are a short flight into Cancún.

Friday dinner is a table booked for all of us, and each woman orders her own. Saturday has a long lunch break with two places nearby I’ll point you to. You eat what your body actually needs, and you pay for it yourself.

The group, the page or the woman who sent you. I like to thank the people who send women my way.

What I’m reading for

Not the hardest story. I’m not going to sit here ranking women’s pain, and I’d be bad at it anyway.

What I’m reading for is readiness. Whether you can already feel the shape of the woman you’ve been performing as, even if you can’t name her yet. Whether something in you has already decided and has just been waiting for a room to say it in.

Three honest sentences will beat three polished paragraphs. Write it fast and send it.

Before you ask

Here are the practical things

Is it really free?

The seat is. Your travel, your lodging and your meals aren’t. That’s the whole catch, and there isn’t another one.

I live in the US. Can I apply?

You can, if you can get yourself here for December 4 and cover the trip. The retreat is built for women already living in Mexico, which is why there’s no lodging in it. If a flight into Cancún in early December is realistic for you, apply.

What if I win and then can’t come?

Tell me the moment you know, and the seat goes to the next woman. Please don’t hold a chair you can’t sit in. In a circle of six, an empty one is felt by everybody.

Will my answers be shared?

No. I read them and nobody else does. If something you wrote moves me and I’d like to quote a line of it publicly, I’ll ask you first, and you can say no without it costing you anything.

Does everyone find out which seat was sponsored?

Only her first name, and only if she’s happy for me to say it. Inside the room, nobody needs to know which seat was sponsored unless she tells them herself.

Is this therapy?

This is identity work led by a mentor. It sits alongside therapy and it doesn’t replace it. If you’re in the middle of something acute, say so when you apply and I’ll be honest with you about whether December is the right month.

What happens in the twenty five days after?

One recording and one piece of work a day, fifteen to twenty minutes, in a private channel. Christmas sits inside it and the audio waits for you. The last day lands on New Year’s Eve, and that’s the day you decide.

Can I apply if I applied for something of yours before?

Yes. Apply again and tell me what’s changed since.
